  • Deletion first enters the visible recycle bin and keeps the photo; recovery cancels soft deletion. Metadata and Storage files in the current account's recycle bin will be permanently deleted only after confirming the clearing; partial can be retried at any stage and will not be considered successful.

Ordinary unchanged coordinate editing retains the existing group; failure/partial retains the old group and time, and never zeroes, splices or silent refreshes.
